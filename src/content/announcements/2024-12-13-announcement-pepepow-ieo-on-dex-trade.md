---
**IEO Details**
**:date: IEO Period:**
**2024/12/19 to 2025/01/30**
**IEO Price:**
**0.00002 USDT/PEPEPOW**
**Tokens for Sale:**
**200,000,000 PEPEPOW**
---
**Why This Price and Cap?**
- The price **0.00002 USDT** reflects an **average between the fluctuating market price** (ranging from 0.00001 to 0.00003 USDT).
- It is carefully chosen to **minimize impact on the current market price** while offering a reasonable entry point for IEO participants.
- The cap of **200 million PEPEPOW** is set to balance demand and avoid oversupply, protecting the market from unnecessary price drops.
---
**What Happens if the IEO Doesn’t Succeed?**
- If the soft cap is not met, the IEO will conclude, and the **PEPEPOW/USDT trading market will open**.
- A total of **112.5 million PEPEPOW** from the funds raised will be allocated toward the listing fee.
