The PEPEPOW community has received confirmation from Dex-Trade that the exchange will **begin the delisting process for PEPEW** due to insufficient trading activity.

Trading on Dex-Trade will be discontinued. Users will be notified directly by the exchange and will be able to withdraw their funds safely within the withdrawal period provided.

Community members are advised to **check their balances** on Dex-Trade and follow the official withdrawal instructions issued by the exchange.

Despite this development, work on the PEPEPOW ecosystem continues, including the Hoohash proof-of-work upgrade, testnet rollout, and ongoing infrastructure improvements.

The community will continue to explore alternative exchange listings and long-term liquidity solutions.
