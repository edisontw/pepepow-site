## [MANDATORY UPGRADE: PePe-core v2.8.1.1]

**Release:** [v2.8.1.1 on GitHub](https://github.com/MattF42/PePe-core/releases/tag/v2.8.1.1)
**Deadline:** All users must upgrade *before* **Monday, July 14, 2025, 18:00 GMT** or you will not be able to connect to mainnet.

### What’s New?

- **Protocol bump to 70520**
- **SPORK18:**
  Enforces the new protocol version from July 14, 2025, ensuring everyone is on the latest software.
- **SPORK21:**
  Adds the ability to *temporarily freeze* coins at specific wallet addresses (max 4 weeks, not burned, only blocked from spending).
- **SPORK signing key:**
  Currently held by Foztor, planned to move to multi-signature (multi-sig) control soon.
- **String-enabled SPORK support**

### Notes for Masternode Owners

- **You must restart your masternode after upgrading.**

### Freeze Feature (SPORK21)

- **No addresses are frozen right now.**
- Any freeze will require DAO consensus and multi-signature approval in the future.
- Frozen coins are *not lost*; once the freeze expires, they are spendable again.

**Please upgrade ASAP!**
More detailed documentation will follow.
