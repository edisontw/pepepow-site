## PEPEW Wallet v1.0.0 Released for Android

The first public version of PEPEW Wallet for Android is now available.

**Download:**  
[PEPEW Wallet v1.0.0 on GitHub](https://github.com/edisontw/pepepow-android-wallet-v2/releases/tag/v1.0.0)

PEPEW Wallet is a lightweight, non-custodial Android wallet for PEPEPOW. Unlike a traditional desktop wallet, it does not need to download and synchronize the complete blockchain.

Recovery words, seed material, private keys, and transaction signing remain locally on the user’s device. The PEPEW Light service is used only for balance and transaction-history queries, UTXO lookup, and broadcasting transactions that have already been signed locally.

### Main Characteristics

- Non-custodial wallet architecture
- Lightweight operation without full blockchain synchronization
- Local private-key storage and transaction signing
- Fast balance and transaction-history access
- Android-focused mobile interface

The Android wallet may be used alongside the browser-based PEPEW Light Wallet:

<https://light.pepepow.net/wallet/>

### Safety Reminder

This is the first public release. Users should securely record and protect their recovery words, test the wallet with a small amount first, and avoid sharing recovery words or private keys with any website, service, or community member.

Users who encounter bugs, device compatibility issues, or unexpected behavior are encouraged to report them through the GitHub repository or PEPEPOW community channels. Suggestions for future improvements are also welcome.
