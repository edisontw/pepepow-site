We're thrilled to announce that PePePow has successfully overcome a significant challenge during our switch to the XelisV2-pepew algorithm. The blockchain paused for a while, but thanks to the swift actions of Foztor and our developers and the valuable feedback from the community, we've resolved the issue.
**What's New?**
- Updated Wallet Released: A new wallet update has been released. Please update your node wallet to the latest version here: [PePePow Wallet Update](https://github.com/MattF42/PePe-core/releases). Pool operators are also requested to update their software accordingly.
**Mining Resources:**
- Mining Software:
- SRBMiner: [Download Here](https://github.com/doktor83/SRBMiner-Multi/releases/)
- **CPUMiner (Foztor's Version):** [Download Here](https://github.com/MattF42/pepew-cpu-miner/releases)
**Supported Pools:**
- Community Pool: [community-pool.pepepow.org](https://community-pool.pepepow.org)
- zpool: [zpool.ca](http://zpool.ca/)
- M4P: [M4P PPLNS](https://mining4people.com/pool/pepecoin-pplns) | [M4P Solo](https://mining4people.com/pool/pepecoin-solo)
**Important Notes:**
- CPU Mining Only: Currently, only CPU mining is supported. GPU mining software will be released soon.
We want to extend our sincere thanks to the developers, mining software creators, pool operators, and community members for their unwavering support. Together, we’ve ensured PePePow continues to thrive!
Thank you,
