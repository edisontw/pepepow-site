Komodo Wallet

Thanks to everyone for their participation; the vote has concluded.

The DEV team at Komodo has asked that we hold off on any funding; we will get the green light once they have completed a comprehensive network test.

The final integration is expected to take 3–4 weeks, but will be communicated as things progress.

Exchanges

The community has requested new listings, and after reading through the suggestions, the following candidates have been highlighted:

- GravieX, LBank, MEXC, Bitrue, Hotcoin, Gate.io, Mercatox, DexTrade, BitMart, KiiroCex, BitGet, TradeOgre

Voting is live (Discord, Telegram) and will conclude in 5 days. You can choose more than one, and to make things interesting, the vote will only show the results at the end. The team will pursue the two exchanges with the highest votes, confirm costs, and start crowd funding soon after.
