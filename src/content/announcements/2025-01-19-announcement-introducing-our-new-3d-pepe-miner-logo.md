Dear PEPEPOW Community,
We are excited to share the results of our logo vote! The new logo—a stunning 3D design of Pepe the Frog as a cheerful miner, complete with a yellow helmet, glowing headlamp, and pickaxe—perfectly reflects PEPEPOW’s innovative spirit and hardworking ethos.
What’s Next?
Logo Rollout: Over the coming weeks, we will replace the old logo across all official platforms, including the website, social media, wallets, and explorer. If you notice any websites or platforms that have not been updated, please be patient and kindly help us inform them.
Community Engagement: In addition to the new logo, we are now inviting the community to vote for a new slogan to further strengthen our brand identity. The shortlisted slogans are:
"Mining the Future, One Block at a Time."
"Built to Meme, Built to Last."
"Powering Innovation, Securing Tomorrow."
"From Memes to Momentum."
"Mining Strength, Forging Community."
Cast your vote and help us choose the slogan that best represents PEPEPOW’s vision!

How should our brand colors, typography, and overall style evolve?

Thank you for your continued support and enthusiasm! Let’s shape the future of PEPEPOW together.
