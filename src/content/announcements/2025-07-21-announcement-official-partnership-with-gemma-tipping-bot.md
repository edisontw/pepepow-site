Big News for PEPEPOW!
We're thrilled to announce our official partnership with **Gemma Tipping Bot** – the new tipping solution for Discord communities!
Months back we discussed Tip.CC integration and many of you were really into this idea, however it turned out to be too expensive for PEPEW at the time. and not worth it for some users. Well I managed to get a great deal with Gemma's dev for 1/5th of what Tip.CC costs ( just $100, we were thinking of using the dev fee for this, poll and vote if you agree!)
Regardless, the dev kindly agreed to integrate us before we payed the fee. This bot has almost the exact same functionality as Tip.cc (and lots more in the works), and just like the new exchange, shows great promise. Hopefully we can both grow together
As part of our ongoing mission to expand PEPEPOW, utility is very important and tipping is a fun, engaging, social activity we can all do (I've seen it do wonders for other communities).
**Gemma now supports $PEPEPOW directly in Discord**. That means:
Instantly tip $PEPEPOW to your friends and community
Run giveaways and rewards in style
No complicated setup
Invite Gemma Bot to your server and start tipping PEPEPOW today!
