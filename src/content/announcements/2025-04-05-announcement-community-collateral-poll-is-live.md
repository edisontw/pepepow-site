**Community Collateral Poll is Live!**
Following recent discussions in our community, we’re now holding a **two-week vote** to decide the future of **masternode collateral requirements**.
Cast your vote here:
[Voting Link](https://discord.com/channels/1206789719825055754/1208677848353865728/1357668919405711513)
We’re asking: **What collateral model should PEPEPOW adopt for masternodes going forward?**
If the community chooses to move away from the current 10M-only model, a **hardfork will be required** to implement the change.
**Note:** This vote does *not* affect the current block reward split between miners and masternodes — that has already been finalized and will remain unchanged.
**About Tiered Options:**
If you vote for a tiered collateral model (**e.g**., 10M, 50M, 100M), higher collateral nodes will be **rewarded more frequently**. For example, under a proportional model, a 50M node would receive rewards **5x more often** than a 10M node.
Your voice matters — help shape the future of PEPEPOW!
